Getting Started With Home Care

Signs that care could help, how to start, and where we work.

How do I know when my parent needs in-home care?

Common signs include difficulty with bathing, dressing, meal preparation, medication reminders, mobility, or maintaining a safe home environment. Increased forgetfulness, isolation, falls, or changes in daily routines may also indicate that additional support would be helpful.

Dedicated Care Living helps families evaluate their loved one’s needs and develop a personalized non-medical care plan that supports safety, independence, and quality of life.

How quickly can home care services begin?

We understand that families sometimes need care urgently, especially following a hospitalization or unexpected change in health.

Dedicated Care Living works to arrange services as quickly as possible, depending on caregiver availability, scheduling requirements, and the client’s individual needs. Contact our team to discuss your situation and potential start dates.

Do you offer a free in-home consultation?

We welcome the opportunity to discuss your family’s needs, explain our services, and determine whether Dedicated Care Living is the right fit. Contact our team to learn about the consultation and assessment process.

Can I arrange home care for my parent if I live out of town?

Absolutely. Many families coordinate care for aging parents while living in another city or state.

Dedicated Care Living works with authorized family members to develop care plans, coordinate schedules, and maintain communication regarding their loved one’s care.

What areas does Dedicated Care Living serve?

Dedicated Care Living provides non-medical home care throughout Los Gatos, San Jose, Campbell, Saratoga, and surrounding Santa Clara County communities, including Morgan Hill and Gilroy.

Contact us to confirm caregiver availability in your specific location.

Services and Scheduling

What we provide and how schedules work.

What services does Dedicated Care Living provide?

We provide personalized non-medical home care services, including companionship, bathing and dressing assistance, mobility support, meal preparation, light housekeeping, medication reminders, transportation, errands, dementia care, respite care, and assistance following hospitalization or surgery.

Our services are designed to help clients remain comfortable, safe, and independent at home.

What is the difference between home care and home health care?

Home care generally focuses on non-medical assistance with daily living, such as bathing, dressing, companionship, meal preparation, and mobility support.

Home health care involves skilled medical services, such as nursing care, wound treatment, and certain therapies, typically ordered by a healthcare provider.

Dedicated Care Living specializes in non-medical home care and can complement services provided by medical professionals.

Do you provide dementia and Alzheimer’s care?

Yes. Dedicated Care Living provides personalized support for individuals living with Alzheimer’s disease and other forms of dementia.

Our caregivers can assist with daily routines, companionship, personal care, safety awareness, and activities appropriate to the client’s abilities and preferences.

We emphasize consistency, patience, compassion, and communication with family members.

Do you provide care after surgery or hospitalization?

Yes. We help individuals transition home following surgery, hospitalization, or rehabilitation.

Caregivers can assist with daily activities, meal preparation, mobility support, transportation, medication reminders, and maintaining a safe and comfortable home environment.

Our services are non-medical and do not replace skilled nursing or rehabilitation services.

Do you provide overnight, live-in, or 24-hour home care?

Dedicated Care Living can help families explore extended-hour, overnight, live-in, and 24-hour care arrangements, depending on individual needs and caregiver availability.

We work with families to determine an appropriate schedule and staffing arrangement.

Can home care schedules change as my parent’s needs change?

Yes. We understand that care needs can evolve over time.

Dedicated Care Living works with families to adjust care plans and schedules as circumstances change, subject to caregiver availability and service arrangements. We strive to have a “team” of caregivers to allow for adequate coverage.

Do you provide transportation to medical appointments and errands?

Yes. Transportation and accompaniment may be included in a client’s personalized care plan.

Our caregivers can help clients attend appointments, run errands, shop for groceries, and participate in appropriate community activities, subject to transportation arrangements and applicable safety policies.

A valid DL is required. DCL carries insurance for caregivers who provide transportation.

Caregivers and Quality of Care

Who your loved one will meet, and how we choose them.

How do you screen and select caregivers?

Dedicated Care Living emphasizes caregiver qualifications, experience, professionalism, and compassion.

Our hiring and onboarding processes include applicable background screening and required training. We also consider a caregiver’s experience, communication skills, and ability to meet each client’s specific needs.

We follow the guidelines set by the CA Department of Social Services.

Will my parent have the same caregiver every visit?

We recognize that consistency is especially important for seniors and individuals experiencing memory changes.

Whenever possible, Dedicated Care Living strives to maintain consistent caregiver assignments while accommodating scheduling needs, caregiver availability, and changes in care requirements.

What happens if our regular caregiver is sick or unavailable?

If a scheduled caregiver becomes unavailable, our team works to identify an appropriate replacement and communicate scheduling changes as promptly as possible.

Replacement coverage depends on caregiver availability and the client’s specific care needs.

Can we meet a caregiver before services begin?

We understand the importance of finding a caregiver who is a good fit for your loved one’s personality, preferences, and needs.

We welcome discussions about caregiver compatibility and can explain available introduction and matching options during the care planning process.

We provide a caregiver bio prior to starting care and can arrange for the family to meet the caregiver (e.g., virtually, by phone, or in person if desired).

What makes Dedicated Care Living different from other home care agencies?

Dedicated Care Living combines compassionate, personalized home care with healthcare-informed leadership.

Our owner, Kevin McClenahan, brings more than 25 years of experience as a physical therapist, providing a valuable perspective on mobility, fall prevention, home safety, and maintaining independence.

We also have access to a Nursing & Healthcare Advisor who can provide professional insight to our leadership team when appropriate.

As a locally owned agency, we emphasize personal attention, caregiver quality, consistent communication, and individualized care plans.

Our philosophy is simple: Thoughtful Care From the Heart.

Pricing and Payment

What affects cost, minimum hours, and insurance.

How much does in-home care cost in Los Gatos and San Jose?

The cost of home care depends on the services requested, the number of hours needed, the schedule, and the level of assistance required.

Dedicated Care Living provides personalized pricing based on each family’s situation. Contact us to discuss your needs and receive a care estimate.

Is there a minimum number of hours required for home care?

Minimum scheduling requirements may apply depending on the type of care and service arrangement. We find that 20 hours/week is a good starting point to be able to source a consistent, reliable caregiver.

Our team can explain current scheduling options and help determine a plan that meets your family’s needs.

Does Medicare or health insurance pay for non-medical home care?

Medicare generally does not cover ongoing custodial or non-medical home care, such as companionship, bathing assistance, or routine help with daily living.

Some long-term care insurance policies may provide benefits for eligible home care services, depending on the policy.

Dedicated Care Living can help families understand the documentation associated with their home care services, although coverage decisions are made by the insurance provider.

Do you accept long-term care insurance?

We work with families who have long-term care insurance and can provide appropriate service documentation to support their claims.

Coverage, reimbursement requirements, benefit limits, and payment arrangements depend on the individual’s insurance policy.

We encourage families to verify their benefits directly with their insurer.

Staying informed when you cannot be there.

How will I know how my parent is doing when I’m not there?

We understand how important peace of mind is for families, especially those who cannot visit regularly.

Dedicated Care Living emphasizes communication between our team, caregivers, clients, and authorized family members. We work with families to establish appropriate communication expectations and provide updates regarding care-related concerns or meaningful changes in needs.

Our goal is to help families feel informed, supported, and confident in their loved one’s care.
