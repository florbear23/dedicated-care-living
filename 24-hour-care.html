How does 24-hour care work?

24-hour care uses rotating caregivers on 8- or 12-hour shifts. They stay awake and available throughout, so someone is always in the home.

What is the difference between 24-hour care and live-in care?

24-hour care uses rotating caregivers on 8- or 12-hour shifts, who stay awake and available throughout. Live-in care is one caregiver staying in the home for an extended period, including overnight. It is for clients who need companionship, help with daily activities and occasional nighttime support, but not continuous awake supervision. We help each family choose based on needs, preferences and budget.
