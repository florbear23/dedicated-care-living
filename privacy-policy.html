At Dedicated Care Living (“DCL,” “we,” “our,” or “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. We understand that choosing in-home care for yourself or a loved one is an important and personal decision.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, contact our team, request services, or otherwise interact with Dedicated Care Living.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following information:

Personal and Contact Information

  • Name, email address, telephone number, and mailing address
  • Preferred method of communication
  • Information provided through website forms, telephone calls, emails, or text messages

Care-Related Information

  • General information about a prospective client's care needs
  • Requested services, schedules, and location of care
  • Mobility, memory, personal care, and other support needs voluntarily shared with us
  • Family or emergency contact information

Website and Technical Information

Our website and authorized service providers may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, device information, and operating system
  • Website pages visited and interactions with our website
  • Referral sources and general website usage information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

Employment-Related Information

If you apply for a position with Dedicated Care Living, we may collect your contact information, employment history, qualifications, and other information necessary to evaluate your application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests for home care services
  • Schedule consultations and care assessments
  • Develop and coordinate personalized non-medical care services
  • Communicate with clients, prospective clients, and authorized family members
  • Provide customer service and respond to concerns
  • Process administrative, billing, and service-related matters
  • Evaluate employment applications
  • Improve our website, marketing, and customer experience
  • Maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Protect our clients, employees, business, and information systems

We seek to collect and use only information reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes.

3. Care-Related and Sensitive Information

We recognize that individuals and families may share personal or sensitive information when requesting home care services.

We use such information to evaluate care needs, coordinate appropriate services, communicate with authorized individuals, and support our business operations.

We take reasonable steps to limit access to sensitive information to individuals and service providers who need it for authorized purposes.

Dedicated Care Living provides non-medical home care services. Any additional privacy obligations applicable to particular records or activities will be addressed in accordance with applicable law.

Please avoid submitting Social Security numbers, detailed medical records, financial account numbers, or other highly sensitive information through general website contact forms unless we have specifically provided an appropriate method for doing so.

4. How We Share Information

We do not disclose personal information to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes without appropriate authorization.

We may share information when reasonably necessary with:

  • Authorized DCL employees and caregivers involved in providing or coordinating services
  • Technology providers supporting website hosting, communications, scheduling, customer relationship management, and business operations
  • Payment processors, accountants, and other professional service providers
  • Healthcare professionals or other parties when appropriately authorized or otherwise permitted by law
  • Government agencies or legal authorities when required by applicable law
  • Other parties with your consent or at your direction

We expect service providers handling personal information on our behalf to use appropriate confidentiality and security protections.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Search Console to understand how visitors find and use the website, so we can improve it. These tools may use cookies and similar technologies.

Information collected may include website interactions, browser and device information, approximate location, and referral sources.

We do not use Google Ads remarketing, the Meta Pixel, or other advertising tracking technologies on this website.

You can manage certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

6. Telephone Calls, Text Messages, and Email

When you contact Dedicated Care Living by telephone, email, or text message, we may retain relevant communications and associated contact information for customer service, scheduling, quality assurance, recordkeeping, and follow-up.

Telephone calls may be recorded or processed using communications technology where permitted by law and subject to applicable notice and consent requirements.

If you provide your telephone number, we may contact you regarding your inquiry, requested services, scheduling, or existing care arrangements.

We will obtain any consent required by applicable law before sending automated or promotional text messages. Where applicable, you may opt out of promotional texts by replying STOP or following the instructions provided.

We do not require consent to receive promotional text messages as a condition of purchasing home care services.

7. Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If you believe information you shared with us has been compromised, please contact us promptly.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, maintain appropriate business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect legitimate business interests.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, the nature of the relationship, and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to delete, destroy, or de-identify it.

9. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have rights concerning their personal information under applicable California privacy laws.

Depending on the law and circumstances, these rights may include requesting access to personal information, requesting correction or deletion, and exercising applicable choices regarding certain uses or disclosures.

Some rights are subject to exceptions, verification requirements, and legal limitations.

To submit a privacy-related inquiry or request, contact:

Dedicated Care Living
Email: support@dedicatedcareliving.com

We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law.

We will not unlawfully discriminate against individuals for exercising applicable privacy rights.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independently operated websites. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any third-party services they use.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended primarily for adults seeking information about home care services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 through our website without appropriate parental or legal guardian authorization.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, technology, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When updates are made, we will revise the effective date at the top of this policy.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
