How quickly can home care services begin?

We understand that families sometimes need care urgently, especially following a hospitalization or unexpected change in health.

Dedicated Care Living works to arrange services as quickly as possible, depending on caregiver availability, scheduling requirements, and the client’s individual needs. Contact our team to discuss your situation and potential start dates.

Do you offer a free in-home consultation?

We welcome the opportunity to discuss your family’s needs, explain our services, and determine whether Dedicated Care Living is the right fit. Contact our team to learn about the consultation and assessment process.

How do I know when my parent needs in-home care?

Common signs include difficulty with bathing, dressing, meal preparation, medication reminders, mobility, or maintaining a safe home environment. Increased forgetfulness, isolation, falls, or changes in daily routines may also indicate that additional support would be helpful.

Dedicated Care Living helps families evaluate their loved one’s needs and develop a personalized non-medical care plan that supports safety, independence, and quality of life.

What areas does Dedicated Care Living serve?

Dedicated Care Living provides non-medical home care throughout Los Gatos, San Jose, Campbell, Saratoga, and surrounding Santa Clara County communities, including Morgan Hill and Gilroy.

Contact us to confirm caregiver availability in your specific location.

Do you provide transportation to medical appointments and errands?

Yes. Transportation and accompaniment may be included in a client’s personalized care plan.

Our caregivers can help clients attend appointments, run errands, shop for groceries, and participate in appropriate community activities, subject to transportation arrangements and applicable safety policies.

A valid DL is required. DCL carries insurance for caregivers who provide transportation.

How much does in-home care cost in Los Gatos and San Jose?

The cost of home care depends on the services requested, the number of hours needed, the schedule, and the level of assistance required.

Dedicated Care Living provides personalized pricing based on each family’s situation. Contact us to discuss your needs and receive a care estimate.
